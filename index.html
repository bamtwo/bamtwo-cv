About

Currently: Open to collaborations

I grew up on an archipelago where the boundary between land and ocean was never fixed — where weather dictated connectivity, and the machines that washed ashore taught me more than any classroom. I learned to code by reverse-engineering firmware on salvaged industrial drones. That experience shaped everything: I see software not as a product but as an ecology. Systems that breathe, adapt, and form unexpected relationships.

My work moves between deep-sea acoustic data systems, generative urban infrastructure, AI-assisted ecological modelling, and cryptographic protocol design. I build tools for creative communities — distributed, open, and designed for a post-scarcity future that hasn't arrived yet. I believe the most interesting technology feels organic but precise: what happens when a mycologist learns to code.

I am not interested in disruption for its own sake. I am interested in emergence — the moment when simple rules generate irreducible complexity. That's where I live. null/signal since 2009. Still listening.
